## ⚠️ TWO FINDINGS THAT CHANGE HOW YOU USE THIS SET

### 1. This is not one room — it is at least two units

The brief assumes a single room page. The photographs do not support that.

- **78 photos are one unit**, identified by its own door plaque as **`K3v Shinjuku Gyoen 503`** (IMG_6331, IMG_6332). Wood-slat blind kitchen, black fridge, red Tiger toaster, fish-print kitchen runner, round oak table, teal sofa, twin beds with duck/ice-cream cushions, geometric wall print.
- **6 photos are a different unit**, plaque reads **`K3v Shinjuku Gyoen 305`** (IMG_6374, IMG_6375). Its washroom is visibly different: black wall cabinet, INAX square vessel basin, grey stone-look counter, black tapware. These are IMG_6369, 6370, 6371, 6372, 6374, 6375.

**Action:** do not mix the 305 washroom photos into the 503 room gallery. A guest who books 503 and arrives to the 503 washroom (white Panasonic-style vanity, IMG_6338) will feel misled. Put the 305 images on a 305 page, or hold them until 305 is shot fully.

### 2. The set has structural gaps and one unusable file

- **No exterior, no entrance/facade, no neighbourhood or Shinjuku Gyoen photo.** For a property whose entire selling proposition is "新宿御苑前で一番予約したくなる", there is nothing here for the location story. This must be a reshoot.
- **No genkan / entry storage, no bed-made-up-for-sleeping (turned-down) shot, no daylight-with-curtains-open living shot.**
- **IMG_6333 is rotated 90°** (cutlery drawer shot lying on its side). Unusable until re-exported with correct EXIF orientation. Its content is duplicated properly by IMG_6334/6335, so simply drop it.
- **Toilet photos (6 of them) all show discoloured grout** on the white square tile. This is the single biggest cleanliness liability in the set. Recommendation: re-clean and reshoot, or publish no toilet photo at all. A missing toilet photo costs nothing; a dirty-grout toilet photo costs bookings.

## PART 4 — BEST HERO IMAGE

### **IMG_6323**

**Why it works as the first image.** A hero has one job: in under two seconds, answer "what exactly am I booking, and do I want it?" IMG_6323 answers both. The frame reads left to right as kitchen → dining table with four chairs → TV → open doorway with a made bed. A viewer arriving cold understands within a glance that this is a multi-room apartment with a real kitchen that sleeps a group — which is precisely the argument for booking direct rather than a hotel room.

**What the guest sees immediately.** Warm wood flooring running the depth of the frame; a round oak table with four seats, already set with a plant and a leather pouch; a white kitchen with sink, IH hob and oven; a black fridge and microwave; a large flat screen; and through the doorway, white bed linen.

**What it communicates.** Total floor plan and circulation; group capacity; genuine self-catering capability; separation of sleeping from living; floor and cabinetry materials; daylight from two directions; and — through the plant, the pouch and the patterned kitchen runner — the "living感 / チープシック" tone the client specified, rather than a sterile high-end hotel look.

**Why it beats the other candidates.**
- **vs IMG_6317** — 6317 is marginally better at pure layout (both bedroom doors visible), but its composition is busier and the clock and door frames compete for attention. 6323 is the cleaner emotional read. Use 6317 as gallery image two.
- **vs IMG_6326 / IMG_6327** — same viewpoint, but a spindle chair-back sits in the centre of the frame, splitting the composition and partially masking the table. Fatal for a hero.
- **vs IMG_6329** — excellent depth, but a third of the frame is a blank corridor wall. Right for a portrait mobile hero, wrong for a desktop hero.
- **vs IMG_6325** — roughly 40% ceiling, with two blown-out ceiling fixtures. Poor exposure discipline for a lead image.
- **vs IMG_6305** — the strongest single image in the set on aesthetics alone, but it is a bedroom. Leading with a bedroom hides the kitchen and the multi-room plan, which are the actual differentiators. It belongs at gallery position three.
- **vs IMG_6279 / IMG_6291** — the most "photographic" images here (shallow focus on the plant), and exactly the trap the brief warns about: attractive, but they communicate almost nothing about the room.

## APPENDIX — METHOD

Every file in IMG_6279–IMG_6375 present in the folder was inspected individually as an image (84 files), not judged from filenames. Each was then compared against the others in its category to resolve redundancy before ranking. Weighting followed the brief: conversion potential 25%, spatial communication 20%, composition 15%, lighting 10%, guest experience 10%, interior appeal 10%, information value 5%, differentiation 5%.
